THOMAS / 01

Blackberry + Vanilla
Sparkling energy · 250 ml can design

The concept

THOMAS / 01 is a fictional sparkling drink, created to show how one product can carry a seasonal story from first glance to final frame.

Identity
An invented beverage brand with a quiet, spaced wordmark and a numbered first edition: 01.
Flavour direction
Blackberry and vanilla: a dark, fruity note softened by warm vanilla. It sets the colour and mood of the campaign rather than describing a real recipe.
Packaging
Deep plum with a soft lilac ribbon and warm ivory type, kept minimal so the colour does the work.
Interactive story
One can carries the whole scroll: a frosted opening, a shift from pale lilac to deep plum, and a still finale. It plays forwards and backwards, adapts to phones and desktops, and works with a keyboard and reduced motion.
