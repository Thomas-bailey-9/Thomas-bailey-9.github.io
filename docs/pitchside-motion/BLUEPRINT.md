## 1. Concept and non-negotiables

Create an exceptionally polished, portfolio-native interactive motion-design case study: five distinct silent 6–8-second seamless-loop football pitchside LED advertisements, viewed in a realistic stadium or crisp studio environment. The motion graphics are always the focus; the environment, camera and interface serve them. Maintain the existing portfolio's typography, navigation, spacing and editorial identity. No unrelated microsite, generic AI-styled dashboard, exaggerated visual effects, fake project results or invented client claims.

## 3. Experience architecture — 10 approved refinements

2. **Adaptive Editorial Campaign Selector:** desktop five-brand horizontal row, active state, Previous/Next; compact scrollable mobile strip; mode persists.
3. **Adaptive Cinematic Mode Switching:** Stadium/Studio segmented control, preserve exact animation frame, selected campaign and playback state; faster mobile/reduced-motion transition.
4. **Adaptive Editorial Playback System:** Play/Pause, Replay, discreet progress, optional Inspect Motion, Play All.
5. **Adaptive Editorial Motion Inspector:** scrubbable timeline, selected key moments and concise rationale, optional expandable timing/easing/construction notes; desktop panel, mobile compact; keyboard accessible.
6. **Adaptive Cinematic Showcase:** Play All follows current mode; restrained campaign-specific stadium angles, clean Studio reel; progress, pause, skip, exit; mobile and reduced-motion simplified.

8. **Hybrid Interactive Editorial Case Study:** showcase first, concise introduction, selected-brand editorial explanation, overall concept/process/animation/final-presentation narrative and stills; no duplication of inspector; accurate unofficial/fictional status.
9. **Hybrid Editorial Process Studio:** challenge, colour/composition/type studies, storyboard frames, stadium comparisons, finished system; occasional purposeful comparison; distinguish real artefacts from retrospective illustrations.
10. **Adaptive Editorial Portfolio Ending:** concise reflection, restrained five-identity arrangement, Revisit Showcase, one *genuine existing* next portfolio project and minimal All Work link; compact mobile, preserve portfolio navigation.

## 4. Final creative review — 10 approved refinements

1. **Adaptive Portfolio-Native Editorial:** match existing site's design and navigation; cinematic dark showcase within editorial portfolio, not a standalone microsite.
2. **Adaptive Cinematic Editorial Transition:** subtle desktop tonal change/stadium reveal; immediate on iPhone; no camera movement for Reduce Motion.
3. **Adaptive Brand-Specific Stadium System:** one physically coherent stadium, campaign-specific restrained lighting, atmosphere and composition; comparable broadcast perspective.
4. **Adaptive Cinematic Camera Direction:** primary camera per brand with near-imperceptible motion, full board legible; fixed broadcast and inspection perspectives; pause/scrub freezes camera; static for Reduce Motion.
5. **Adaptive Living Stadium:** gentle crowd/floodlight/pitchside life, quieter at signature moments and holds; freezes on pause/inspect; mobile simplified, reduced-motion static.
6. **Adaptive Physical LED Detail:** clean normal-distance presentation, close-range realistic pixels, seams, housing, reflections and restrained spill; optional LED Detail within existing inspection controls; return to exact prior state; no colour distortion or flicker.
7. **Adaptive Cinematic Brand Transition:** outgoing settles, atmosphere/camera adjust, incoming opening frame established *before* its timeline starts; title/selector/context synchronise; desktop subtle, mobile fast, reduced-motion instant. Transition time is outside 6–8s ad.
8. **Adaptive Playback Preservation:** suspend rendering off-screen/in inactive tab; preserve campaign, exact frame, mode, camera, manual play/pause, inspector and Play All index; resume only if previously playing.
9. **Adaptive Cinematic Loading:** immediate accurate preview; load ad and core controls first; enhance lighting/crowd/LED later; no silent advancement while loading; functional fallback on slow/unsupported devices.
10. **Adaptive Editorial Discovery:** show ad immediately with “Five campaigns. One pitchside canvas. Explore the motion.”; labelled primary controls; Inspect Motion/LED Detail contextual; no mandatory tour, popups or excessive instructions.

## 6. Behaviour and state contract

Maintain a deterministic campaign animation clock and explicit state for:
- selected campaign and animation position;
- playing / manually paused / suspended / loading;
- Stadium / Studio mode;
- primary / broadcast camera;
- inspector open, key moment and scrub position;
- LED Detail open/closed and return context;
- Play All index, overall progress and completion;
- viewport/tab visibility and Reduce Motion;
- essential vs optional asset readiness.

**Core invariants:**
- Stadium and Studio display the *same animation frame* at any given timeline position.
- Pause freezes ad, camera and environmental movement.
- Mode changes and LED Detail inspection preserve frame and intent.
- Brand switches begin the new campaign at its opening frame; transitions never consume its runtime.
- Off-screen suspension preserves manual pause; do not restart on return.
- Play All plays all five full ads in order, respects current mode, allows pause/skip/exit and stops at the finale.
- Individual ads loop seamlessly; Play All does not automatically loop.
- Advanced effects never block basic usable playback.

## 7. Responsive, accessibility and performance

**Desktop:** spacious editorial composition, readable panoramic player, subtle camera treatments, clear horizontal selector, optional adjacent inspector.

**iPhone:** very short introduction, full uncropped LED composition, touch-friendly scrollable selector, compact controls and inspector, minimal camera movement, lightweight stadium and efficient scrolling. Do not crop essential artwork or force text too small.

**Accessibility:** semantic controls, keyboard operation, visible focus, accessible names, keyboard-accessible scrubbing/key-moment alternatives, sensible focus restoration, legible contrast, no strobing/flicker, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured for camera/environment/transitions. Provide meaningful static presentation of campaign content when motion is reduced.

**Loading/performance:** show accurate preview immediately; prioritise core animation and controls, lazy-load environmental enhancements, suspend rendering off-screen/tab hidden, degrade gracefully if advanced graphics unsupported. Do not pick a rendering framework before inspecting the current portfolio.

## 8. Acceptance tests

- Each campaign's approved headline, palette, animation identity and loop are visibly distinct.
- Each campaign runs ~6–8 seconds, silently, with no hard reset.
- Selected brand updates the selector, creative description and ad together.
- Studio/Stadium mode switch preserves exact frame and playback state.
- Pause, Replay, scrub and key-moment selection are precise.
- LED Detail returns to previous selected frame and context.
- Stadium remains physically consistent; campaign-specific lighting is restrained.
- Background activity and camera freeze on pause/inspection.
- Campaign transition happens outside animation runtime.
- Play All runs PUMA, Heineken, Mastercard, VANTA, ORYN in order, with pause/skip/exit, then stops on the finale.
- Scroll/tab suspension preserves position and manual pause.
- Initial preview and fallback work without heavy stadium effects.
- Mobile and keyboard interaction are usable; Reduce Motion removes nonessential movement.
- Editorial copy identifies the three real-brand concepts as independent/unofficial and the two fictional brands as fictional.
- Closing next-project link points to an actual existing project.
